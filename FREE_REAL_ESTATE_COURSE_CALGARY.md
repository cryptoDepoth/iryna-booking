## 💰 Модуль 2: Стратегии Инвестирования

### 2.1 BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat)

**Канадская специфика:**
- В Канаде **не работает** чистый американский BRRRR
- Причина: **stress test + refinance appraisal** (CMHC rules)
- Рефинансирование даёт только 80% of appraised value (не 100% как в US)

**Модификация для Канады: BRRR-Light**
1. **Buy** под market value (distressed, estate sale)
2. **Rehab** — добавить value (basement suite, kitchen, bathroom)
3. **Rent** — legal suite = higher income
4. **Refinance** — вытащить часть equity, но оставить 20% skin in the game
5. **Repeat** — но медленнее чем в US (нужен больше cash per deal)

**Бесплатные ресурсы:**
- BiggerPockets YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@BiggerPockets
- Reddit r/CanadianInvestor
- CMHC Second Dwelling Unit Guide: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca

### 2.2 House Hacking

**Что это:** Живёшь в одной части дома, сдаёшь другую.

**Канадские варианты:**
- **Basement suite** — Legal secondary suite (проверь zoning!)
- **Garage suite** — Backyard suite (Calgary разрешает)
- **Room-by-room** — AirBnB (проверь bylaws!)
- **Duplex** — Живёшь в одной половине

**Преимущество для newcomers:**
- Нужен только **5% down payment** (CMHC insured) для primary residence
- vs **20%** for pure investment property

**Legal basement suite requirements Calgary:**
- Separate entrance
- Kitchen + bathroom
- Ceiling height 1.95m
- Egress window in bedroom
- Fire separation

**Проверь:** https://www.calgary.ca/content/dam/www/pda/pd/pdfs/bru/bru-legal-secondary-suite-guide.pdf

### 2.3 Flipping

**Канадская реальность:**
- **Capital gains tax** на 50% of profit (не как в US с 1031 exchange)
- **GST** на новую постройку/major renovation
- **Holding costs** (mortgage, property tax, utilities) съедают profit если медленно
- **Success rate**: 60% of flippers lose money or break even (CMHC data)

**Когда flipping работает в Калгари:**
- Buy: Estate sale, foreclosure, distressed
- Hold time: < 3 months
- Renovation budget: < 15% of purchase price
- Sell: Spring market (March-May)

### 2.4 Long-term Buy & Hold

**Самая простая стратегия для Калгари:**
- Купить cashflow-positive property
- Сдавать long-term (не AirBnB — Calgary bylaws strict)
- Платить mortgage чужими деньгами (rent)
- Через 15-25 лет — fully owned asset

**Cashflow rule:**
- Monthly rent ≥ 1% of purchase price (The 1% Rule)
- В Калгари: 0.7-0.9% more realistic
- Example: $400,000 house → rent $3,200/month = cashflow positive

## 🏦 Модуль 3: Ипотека для Newcomers

### 3.1 Основные программы

| Программа | Требования | Преимущество |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **New to Canada Program** | < 5 years in Canada, full-time job | 5% down, competitive rates |
| **Genworth Newcomer 95** | Same as CMHC but Genworth | Alternative to CMHC |
| **Sagen (formerly Genworth)** | Newcomer with work permit | Flexibility |

### 3.2 Стандартные требования (2026)

**Для primary residence:**
- Down payment: **5%** (до $500k), **10%** ($500k-$1M)
- Credit history: **12-24 months** preferred
- Income proof: T4, pay stubs, employment letter
- GDS/TDS ratio: **39%/44%** (Gross Debt Service / Total Debt Service)

**Для investment property:**
- Down payment: **20% minimum** (uninsured)
- Stress test: Qualify at **+2%** above contract rate or 5.25%, whichever is higher
- Rental income: 50-80% counts toward qualification

### 3.3 Креативное финансирование (что реально работает в Канаде)

| Стратегия | Легально? | Когда использовать |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Vendor Take-Back Mortgage (VTB)** | ✅ Да | Seller finances part of purchase |
| **Private Lending** | ✅ Да | Hard money for flip/renovation |
| **Joint Venture (JV)** | ✅ Да | Partner brings cash, you manage |
| **Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)** | ✅ Да | Use equity in existing home |
| **RRSP Home Buyer's Plan** | ✅ Да | Borrow from own RRSP (first-time) |
| **Subject-to** (US strategy) | ❌ Нет | Canadian banks don't allow |
| **Lease-option** | 🟡 Сложно | Rare in Canada, legal issues |

**Бесплатные ресурсы:**

- CMHC Mortgage Calculator: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/en/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-securitization/mortgage-loan-insurance/cmhc-mortgage-calculator

## 📊 Модуль 4: Анализ Объекта (Шаблон)

### 4.1 Быстрый анализ за 10 минут

| Поле | Формула | Пример |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Purchase Price** | Listed price or offer | $450,000 |
| **Closing Costs** | 1.5-2% of purchase price | $6,750-9,000 |
| **Renovation Budget** | Estimate from inspection | $25,000 |
| **Total Investment** | Purchase + Closing + Reno | $481,750 |
| **After Repair Value (ARV)** | Comparable sales | $520,000 |
| **Potential Profit** | ARV - Total Investment | $38,250 |

### 4.2 Cashflow Analysis (для hold)

| Income | |
|--------|---|
| Monthly Rent (main unit) | $2,200 |
| Monthly Rent (basement suite) | $1,200 |
| **Total Monthly Income** | **$3,400** |

| Expenses | |
|----------|---|
| Mortgage Payment (P+I) | $2,400 |
| Property Tax (monthly) | $350 |
| Insurance | $150 |
| Maintenance (5% of rent) | $170 |
| Vacancy (5% of rent) | $170 |
| Property Management (8%) | $272 |
| Utilities (if included) | $0 |
| **Total Monthly Expenses** | **$3,512** |

| Cashflow | |
|----------|---|
| **Monthly Cashflow** | **-$112** (negative!) |
| **Cap Rate** | (Annual Rent - Expenses) / Purchase Price |
| **Cash-on-Cash Return** | Annual Cashflow / Cash Invested |

### 4.3 The 50% Rule (быстрый тест)

**50% of gross rent goes to expenses** (не включая mortgage).

Example:
- Rent: $3,400/month = $40,800/year
- Expenses (50%): $20,400/year = $1,700/month
- Mortgage: $2,400/month
- **Total: $4,100/month > $3,400 income = NEGATIVE**

**Вывод:** Property doesn't cashflow without suite or higher rent.

## 🔍 Модуль 5: Due Diligence Чеклист

### 5.1 Перед покупкой (must-check)

- [ ] **Zoning check** — Can you add secondary suite? (City of Calgary)
- [ ] **Title search** — Liens, easements, encroachments (lawyer)
- [ ] **Property disclosure** — Seller must disclose known defects
- [ ] **Home inspection** — Structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC ($400-600)
- [ ] ** condo docs (if applicable)** — Financials, reserve fund, bylaws
- [ ] **Rental history** — Current tenancies, rent rolls, leases
- [ ] **Tax assessment** — vs purchase price (lower assessment = lower taxes)
- [ ] **Comparable sales** — 3-5 sold in last 90 days within 1km
- [ ] **Neighborhood trends** — Crime, schools, transit, development

### 5.2 Красные флаги (walk away)

- Foundation cracks (structural)
- Aluminum wiring (insurance nightmare)
- Poly-B plumbing (leaks)
- Knob-and-tube wiring (uninsurable)
- Stigma: former grow-op, murder, flood zone
- HOA fees > $500/month (condo)
- Special assessment pending
- Rental restrictions in condo bylaws

## 🏛️ Модуль 7: Налоги и Структура

### 7.1 Как налогообложение работает в Канаде

| Тип дохода | Налог | Комментарий |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| **Rental income** | Personal income tax | Deduct expenses, depreciation (CCA) |
| **Capital gains** | 50% of gain taxable | Principal residence exempt |
| **Flipping profit** | Business income | 100% taxable, GST may apply |
| **Assignment fees** | Business income | Wholesale income |

### 7.2 Структура владения

| Структура | Плюсы | Минусы |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Personal name** | Просто, lowest mortgage rates | Unlimited liability |
| **Joint tenancy** | With spouse/partner | Both liable |
| **Corporation (Holdco)** | Liability protection, tax planning | Higher mortgage rates, complexity |
| **Trust** | Estate planning, privacy | Complex, expensive setup |

**Для первой сделки:** Personal name or joint tenancy. Corporation — после 2-3 properties.

### 7.3 Налоговые вычеты (deductions)

Что можно списать:
- Mortgage interest (not principal)
- Property tax
- Insurance
- Maintenance & repairs
- Property management fees
- Legal & accounting fees
- Home office (if applicable)
- CCA (Capital Cost Allowance) — depreciation

**Нельзя:**
- Principal mortgage payments
- Personal use portion
- Improvements (capitalized, not expensed)

**Бесплатные ресурсы:**
- CRA Rental Income Guide: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4036.html
- SimpleTax/Wealthsimple Tax — free tax filing for low income

## 🎯 Модуль 8: Пошаговый План Первой Сделки

### Фаза 1: Обучение (2-4 недели)

- [ ] Пройти этот курс (бесплатно)
- [ ] Посмотреть 10 часов YouTube (BiggerPockets + Canadian channels)
- [ ] Прочитать CMHC Newcomer Guide
- [ ] Посетить 2-3 open houses (не покупать, просто смотреть)
- [ ] Поговорить с 3 mortgage brokers (бесплатные консультации)
- [ ] Поговорить с 2 realtors (интервью — выбирай кого нанимать)

### Фаза 2: Финансирование (1-2 недели)

- [ ] Получить mortgage pre-approval
- [ ] Знать точно: сколько можешь позволить
- [ ] Save closing costs (~$10-15k)
- [ ] Emergency fund: 3-6 months of expenses

### Фаза 3: Поиск объекта (4-8 недель)

- [ ] Setup MLS alerts (via realtor or Zolo)
- [ ] Анализировать 20+ объектов (не смотреть, а считать)
- [ ] Посетить 5-10 showing
- [ ] Сделать 2-3 offers (вероятность acceptance ~30%)
- [ ] Due diligence: inspection, title, zoning

### Фаза 4: Закрытие (30-60 дней)

- [ ] Remove conditions (financing, inspection)
- [ ] Lawyer handles closing
- [ ] Get insurance
- [ ] Setup utilities
- [ ] Prepare for tenants (if renting immediately)

### Фаза 5: Управление (ongoing)

- [ ] Screen tenants (credit check, references, employment)
- [ ] Sign lease (Alberta standard lease: https://www.alberta.ca/standard-residential-lease-agreement)
- [ ] Collect rent
- [ ] Maintain property
- [ ] Review annually: refinance? sell? hold?
