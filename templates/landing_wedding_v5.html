Wedding Photography Questions

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Calgary?
Wedding photography in Calgary starts at $640 + GST for small weddings (2 hours, 50 edited photos + all originals). Wedding day coverage is $1,280 for 4 hours or $1,920 for 6 hours; the Full Day Premium (8 hours) is $2,600.
Do you travel outside Calgary for weddings?
Yes, I cover weddings in Canmore, Banff, and the Bow Valley area. Travel fees apply for destinations beyond 50km from Calgary — contact me for a custom quote.
How many photos do we get from a Calgary wedding?
A small wedding (2h) includes 50 professionally edited photos plus all original unedited images. Wedding day coverage (4-6h) includes 150+ edited photos.
When do we receive our wedding photos?
You receive a preview gallery within 48 hours. The full gallery is ready within 2 weeks. All photos delivered via private online gallery with download access.
Can we meet before booking?
Absolutely. I offer a free 15-minute video call to discuss your vision, preferred locations, and timeline.
