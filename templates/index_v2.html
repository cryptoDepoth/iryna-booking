Quick answers

What should we wear?
I send a styling guide after booking — soft tones, layered textures, nothing with big logos.
What if it rains?
We reschedule for free, no questions asked. I check the forecast 24h before and reach out.
Can I bring our dog?
Always yes — just let me know on the form so I plan the spot.
How does the deposit work?
The deposit is non-refundable but transferable to another session if you give 7+ days notice. The remaining balance is due on session day.
When do I get the photos?
Within 48 hours for mini sessions, 2 weeks for full sessions. You get a private gallery with download.
