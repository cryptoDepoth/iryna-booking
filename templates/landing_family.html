Family Photography Questions

How much does family photography cost in Calgary?
Family sessions in Calgary start at $340 + GST for 1 hour with 25 edited photos and all original images included. Mini family sessions run $180-$240.
What should we wear for family photos?
Soft tones, layered textures, and clothes without big logos work best. I send a complete styling guide after booking with color palette suggestions.
Can we bring our dog?
Absolutely! Just mention it when booking. Many Calgary parks are dog-friendly and make wonderful backdrops.
Where are the best places for family photos?
Riley Park, Confederation Park, Prince's Island Park, and Bow River pathways are favourites. I also know hidden spots with great light.
How long does a family session take?
A full family session is 1 hour — enough for group shots, individual portraits, and candid moments. Mini sessions are 20 minutes.
