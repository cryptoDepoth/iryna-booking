Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This booking site is designed to collect only what is needed to reserve and confirm a photography session.

This site uses Meta Pixel and the Google Ads tag to measure ad visits and verified bookings. These services may use browser identifiers according to your browser and provider settings.

What information is collected

When you reserve a session, the booking form asks for contact and appointment details:

  • name
  • email
  • phone number
  • Instagram handle, if provided
  • selected session, date, time, and payment status

Browser storage and advertising measurement

The site uses limited browser storage for the booking flow and for measuring whether advertising leads to a verified booking:

  • localStorage stores your selected language so pages stay in the same language.
  • sessionStorage stores the temporary reservation countdown during checkout.
  • Meta Pixel and the Google Ads tag receive page, ad-click, checkout, and verified-purchase signals. Purchase events use a transaction ID to prevent duplicate counting.

You can limit or block these technologies in your browser or provider ad settings; the booking form itself will still work.

Payments

Interac e-Transfer is handled through your bank. If you choose card / Apple Pay / Google Pay, checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe may process payment-related data according to Stripe’s own privacy and security policies.

How booking data is used

Your details are used for practical session operations only:

  • holding your selected time slot
  • confirming payment
  • sending session details and reminders
  • managing schedule, calendar, and client communication

Questions or deletion requests

If you want your booking details updated or removed, DM @pashynska.photo.
