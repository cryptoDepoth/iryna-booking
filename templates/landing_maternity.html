Maternity Photography Questions

When is the best time for maternity photos?
The ideal time is between 28-34 weeks of pregnancy. Your belly is beautifully round and you still have the energy to enjoy the session.
How much does maternity photography cost?
Maternity sessions start at $340 + GST for 1 hour with 25 edited photos and all original images included.
What should I wear?
Flowing dresses, form-fitting outfits that highlight your bump, and soft neutral tones work beautifully. I provide a detailed styling guide after booking.
Can my partner join?
Absolutely. Including your partner and children creates meaningful family portraits during this special time.
Studio or outdoor?
Both! Studio sessions are perfect year-round. Outdoor sessions capture golden light and Alberta landscapes. Choose what feels right for you.
