Family Photography Questions

How much does a family photographer cost in Calgary?
Family sessions start at $340 + GST for 1 hour with 25 edited photos and all original images included. Mini family sessions run $180–$240; extended family sessions (1.5 hours, 40 edited photos) are $510.
Where do sessions take place?
Anywhere in Calgary — Bowness Park, Fish Creek, Confederation Park and downtown are favourites. Golden-hour outdoor sessions are most popular; seasonal studio setups are available for minis.
What if our kids don't cooperate?
Totally normal — sessions are playful, not posed. Real laughter, snack breaks and running around are welcome: the candid in-between moments usually become the favourites.
How many photos do we get and how fast?
A 1-hour session includes 25 edited photos plus every original image. Preview gallery within 48 hours, full gallery within 2 weeks — delivered via private online gallery with print rights.
What should we wear?
Soft neutrals and gentle earth tones photograph beautifully in golden light. After booking you'll receive a detailed styling guide with ideas for every season.
