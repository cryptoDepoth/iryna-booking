Maternity Photography Questions

When is the best time for maternity photos?
The sweet spot is 28–34 weeks: the bump is beautifully round and you still feel comfortable moving through the session. Book around week 20 so we can pick the perfect date.
How much does maternity photography cost?
Maternity sessions start at $340 + GST for 1 hour with 25 edited photos and all original images included. Mini sessions run $180–$240; the Bump to Baby bundle (maternity + newborn) is $600 and saves $80.
What should I wear?
Flowing dresses and soft, form-fitting outfits that celebrate your bump photograph beautifully. After booking you'll receive a detailed styling guide for studio and outdoor light.
Can my partner and kids join?
Absolutely — partner and children are always welcome at no extra charge. Some of the sweetest frames are the ones together.
How many photos do I get and how fast?
A 1-hour session includes 25 edited photos plus every original image. Preview gallery within 48 hours, full gallery within 2 weeks — via private online gallery with print rights.
