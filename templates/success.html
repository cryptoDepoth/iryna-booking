How to Prepare
👗
Wardrobe Soft, coordinating tones — lilac, cream, pastels, or white. Avoid busy patterns and neon.
💇‍♀️
Hair & Makeup Natural, dewy makeup photographs best. Stick with your usual hairstyle.
👟
Footwear Grass may be damp. Bring a spare pair and be ready to go barefoot for some shots.
🧒
Kids & Family Feed children before the session. Bring snacks, water, and a comfort toy.
⏰
Arrive 5 min early Your slot is exactly 20 minutes. Location details sent via DM closer to the date.
