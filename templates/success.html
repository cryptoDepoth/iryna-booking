🪻 How to Prepare for Your Photoshoot

👗
Wardrobe
Wear soft, coordinating tones — lilac, cream, pastels, or white look stunning against the blossoms. Avoid busy patterns, logos, and neon colors.
💇‍♀️
Hair & Makeup
Natural, dewy makeup photographs best. Style your hair as you normally would, but avoid brand-new hairstyles you've never tried before.
👟
Footwear
Grass may be damp in May. Bring a second pair of shoes and be ready to go barefoot for some romantic shots if you're comfortable.
🧒
Kids & Family
Feed children before the session. Bring snacks, water, and a small comfort toy. A well-rested child = magical photos.
⏰
Arrive 5 Min Early
Your slot is exactly 20 minutes. Arriving early maximizes your shoot time. Location details will be sent via DM once payment is confirmed.
🌸
Bonus Tip
The lilac backdrop is beautiful but attracts bees. Stay calm — we can work around them, and I always bring a backup location plan.
