## Out-of-repository owner recommendations

These findings are explicit recommendations only. They were not changed by this repository mission.

### `pashynskaphoto.com` duplicate homepage canonical

- **Observed problem:** the apex portfolio domain served a near-duplicate indexable homepage without a canonical.
- **Impact:** search engines may split signals or choose an unintended duplicate.
- **Recommended owner action:** add a self-consistent canonical or a carefully reviewed redirect to the preferred portfolio URL at the Wfolio/Cloudflare owner layer. Preserve required exceptions such as `/disk` and confirm the final redirect map before enabling it.
- **Repository action:** none. This site/configuration is outside the booking repository.

### `www.irynapashynska.com` HTTP 503

- **Observed problem:** the `www` host returned HTTP 503 during the audit.
- **Impact:** visitors and crawlers cannot reliably access the hostname.
- **Recommended owner action:** repair or remove the stale DNS/origin mapping, then choose a permanent redirect to the current preferred domain if the hostname is still owned and intended for use.
- **Repository action:** none. DNS, origin, and external-site ownership are outside this repository.
